## Why BVE Workbench?

Building routes for OpenBVE usually means editing massive `.csv` or `.rw` text files by hand or wrestling with dated tools. **BVE Workbench** is being built from scratch in modern C# to give route creators a faster, cleaner workspace.

* **Native `.csv` & `.rw` support** without fighting syntax errors blind
* **Modern .NET performance** built to handle complex routes smoothly
* **Community-driven** — shaped directly by route builders and C# contributors
