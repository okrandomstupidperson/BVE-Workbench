# Get Involved

BVE Workbench is at the very beginning of development. Whether you write C#, build OpenBVE routes, or just want to test early builds and share feedback, you're welcome to join.

## How to Join the Discord
1. **Join the [Discord Server]([YOUR-DISCORD-INVITE-LINK])**
2. **Fill out the short [Application Form](https://forms.gle/NYmdehKfMj7Wdojo8)**
3. **Open a ticket** in Discord and paste the **7-character code** shown at the end of the form

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What language and framework is this built with?**
C# on modern .NET, licensed under the open-source MIT License.

**Will it support both `.csv` and `.rw` route formats?**
Yes—the core parser is being designed around OpenBVE's standard `.csv` and legacy `.rw` route specifications.

**Is there a download available yet?**
Not yet! Keep an eye on the [Roadmap](roadmap) or join the Discord to test early builds as soon as the first alpha is ready.
